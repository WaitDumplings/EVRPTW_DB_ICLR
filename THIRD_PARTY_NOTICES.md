## Data attribution and modifications

**© OpenStreetMap contributors.** Road and depot source information is available under the [Open Data Commons Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/); see [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This project extracts directed road/access layers and computes path matrices. The tiny generation fixture is a cropped subset and differs from the original full-city graph.

**Microsoft US Building Footprints.** Building geometry is provided by Microsoft under ODbL; see the [official repository and data license](https://github.com/microsoft/USBuildingFootprints). Building candidates are transformed into latent customer/access locations, with public NSI-derived attributes where indicated. No imagery is distributed.

**U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, National Structure Inventory.** Public NSI 2026 Base structure attributes support customer-layer construction. All fields selected by the source adapter are in the [2026 public schema](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/nsi/technicalreferences/2026/technical-documentation). No restricted fields or Data Use Agreement rights are asserted. These modeled structure attributes are not verified building-level observations, and the project does not relicense NSI material under its project license or CC0.

**U.S. Census Bureau.** The full generator uses Census city and block-group geography. [TIGER/Line® Shapefiles](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html). TIGER/Line is a registered trademark of the U.S. Census Bureau. Boundaries are for statistical use and are not legal land descriptions; the Census Bureau is the source, not an endorser of this derived benchmark.

**U.S. Department of Energy, Alternative Fuels Data Center.** Charging infrastructure data are filtered and mapped to road access points; benchmark charging powers may use the documented vehicle cap and missing-power imputation. Consult the [AFDC download terms](https://afdc.energy.gov/data_download) and [disclaimer](https://afdc.energy.gov/disclaimer). Current location/accessibility information should not be inferred from a frozen research snapshot.

**U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.** The generator uses MOVES5-derived reference speed profiles. Citation: USEPA (2024), *Motor Vehicle Emission Simulator: MOVES5*, Office of Transportation and Air Quality, Ann Arbor, MI, November 2024. [Official MOVES page](https://www.epa.gov/moves/latest-version-motor-vehicle-emission-simulator-moves). This distribution does not include the MOVES simulator or assert EPA approval of derived routing profiles.

**Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates.** The 2021 Amazon Last Mile Routing Research Challenge material is used under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). [Dataset and license source](https://registry.opendata.aws/amazon-last-mile-challenges/). Citation: Daniel Merchán, Jatin Arora, Julian Pachon, Karthik Konduri, Matthias Winkenbach, Steven Parks and Joseph Noszek (2022), *2021 Amazon Last Mile Routing Research Challenge: Data Set*, Transportation Science, [doi:10.1287/trsc.2022.1173](https://doi.org/10.1287/trsc.2022.1173).
